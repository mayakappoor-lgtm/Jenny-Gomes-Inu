      Free assessment

      Our questions follow the topics in the Government of Canada’s official Come to Canada tool: your goal, nationality, age, language, family, education, work, funds and job offer. We are not IRCC and this is not an official form.

      How to start

      1. Try the official toolIt is free and takes 10–15 minutes. Open Come to Canada. Results are for reference only and do not affect any decision.
      2. Save your Personal Reference CodeThe tool gives you a code that links your answers to your IRCC online account.
      3. Answer our questions belowBe as accurate as you can. Add your code in the last box.
      4. We review and replyWe explain your options, timelines and the documents you need. See our services or browse jobs meanwhile.

      Your answers go by email to our team. This site stores nothing. Questions? Contact us.
